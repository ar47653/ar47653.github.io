Project Management

For my final project, I'm thinking of presenting about security, more specifically about scams on the internet since it is becoming more relevant and I believe there is a strong need for such education for anyone with online access. I'll most likely do a powerpoint presentation and screen record the presentation as I speak. The bulk of the presentation will be on signs to look out for and what to do when encountering an online scam. When learning about the Gantt chart, I remembered seeing similar charts that project managers created at work. Sometimes there were meetings where a project manager would create their Gantt chart on the spot while asking each department on when they can complete their tasks. This session gave me a lot of insights on the responsibilities of project managers. I noticed that creating a Gantt chart helps you see projects as a whole and allow the tasks to be measurable. Once the project is over, the Gantt chart can help with improving the management process by comparing it with how the project actually went.
