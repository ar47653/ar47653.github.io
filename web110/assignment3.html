I mainly use Gmail for emails, but my first email that I made was with Yahoo Mail. For work I use Microsoft Outlook for email and email client service. It makes sense that email is still used today as the main communication method for many professional settings. It is a great way to keep track and archive any written text or media that has been sent or recieved by anyone. However, one weakness of email is security. There are many instances even in well established companies where private information gets accessed through malicious links or attachment within an email.
